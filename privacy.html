Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us — here is exactly how we handle your data.

📅 Last updated: September 10, 2024

KECHI ("we", "us", "our") operates the KECHI mobile application and website (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (or Google Sign-In credentials)
  • Order information: delivery address, poster customisation details, order history
  • Payment information: processed securely via Razorpay — we do not store your card, UPI, or banking details on our servers
  • Uploaded content: images or files you upload for custom poster designs
  • Device and usage data: app version, device type, crash logs, and basic analytics to improve the Service

2. How We Collect It

  • Directly from you — sign-up forms, checkout, profile, and upload flows
  • Automatically — via Firebase Authentication, Firestore, and Firebase Storage as you use the app
  • From third parties — Google Sign-In (if used) and Razorpay (payment confirmation status only)

3. Why We Use It

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process, print, and deliver your custom poster orders
  • To send order updates and delivery notifications
  • To provide customer support and resolve refund or delivery issues
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

4. Third-Party Sharing

We share only the minimum necessary data with:

  • Razorpay — to process payments securely
  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication, database, storage, and push notifications
  • Delivery partners — your name, address, and phone number, solely to fulfil delivery

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Firebase's secure cloud infrastructure. We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

  • Access or update your personal information from your in-app profile
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of promotional notifications at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@kechi.in.

7. Cookies (Website)

Our website may use minimal cookies for basic analytics and session functionality. No cookies are used for third-party advertising.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, reach us at support@kechi.in or visit our Contact page.
