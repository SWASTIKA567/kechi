Refund & Cancellation Policy

We stand behind our print quality — here is exactly how refunds and cancellations work.

📅 Last updated: September 10, 2024

Since every poster on KECHI is custom-printed for each order, we follow a strict quality-verification process before and during delivery to protect both you and us.

📸 Delivery Verification:

Our delivery partner captures photos of your poster at the time of delivery to confirm its condition before handover. This record is used to verify any damage or quality claims.

1. Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled only before printing or production begins. Once your poster enters production, it cannot be cancelled, as it is made specifically for you.

  • To cancel, go to My Orders in the app and select "Cancel Order" — this option is only visible if production has not started.
  • If you need help, contact us immediately at support@kechi.in.

2. Refund Eligibility

We accept refund requests only in the following situations:

  • Damaged on delivery: The poster arrived visibly damaged or torn (verified by delivery photo).
  • Wrong item: You received a different size, design, or finish than what you ordered.
  • Significant print defect: A colour or print error that is clearly our fault (verified against your uploaded file).

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Errors in the uploaded image (blurriness, wrong file, low resolution) — please review before checkout.
  • Change of mind after production has started.
  • Incorrect address provided by the customer leading to delivery failure.
  • Minor colour variations due to display vs. print differences (natural for all print services).

4. How to Raise a Refund Request

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery at support@kechi.in.
  2. Include your Order ID and clear photos of the issue.
  3. Our team will review and respond within 2 business days.
  4. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

5. Replacement Policy

In eligible cases, we may offer a replacement print instead of a refund. Replacements are dispatched at no additional cost and within the same delivery timeline as a new order.

6. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy, reach us at support@kechi.in or visit our Contact page.
