Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? ▼
Most orders are printed and dispatched within 24 hours. Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on your location in India.
Can I cancel my order? ▼
Yes — but only before production begins. Once printing starts, cancellations are not possible as the poster is custom-made for you.
What file formats do you accept? ▼
We accept JPEG, PNG, and PDF files. For best print quality, use high-resolution files (at least 300 DPI at the intended print size).
My poster arrived damaged. What should I do? ▼
Email us at support@kechi.in within 48 hours of delivery with your Order ID and photos. We'll arrange a replacement or refund promptly.
Which payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets — all processed securely through Razorpay.
Do you ship outside India? ▼
Currently, KECHI delivers across India only. International shipping is on our roadmap — stay tuned!
